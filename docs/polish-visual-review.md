## First-pass required revisions — resolved in the second pass

1. **P1 — decorative loop rock blocks the course and chase camera.** `polishcritic-loop-entry.png` at timer 4.14 shows a huge pale outcrop covering the left and center of the screen and hiding most of Sonic and the road. `src/super-track.js` constructs the loop ledge as `entry.position + entry.right * 48 + (0,-8,30)`, with scale `[20,18,24]`. The world-Z offset is not an offset along the course. The first loop entry is `(160,9,340)` and right is approximately `(0,0,-1)`, making the rock center `(160,1,322)`; its 24 m Z extent reaches into the driving ribbon around Z332–348. Place the outcrops using frame-relative forward/up offsets and on the opposite side from the loop drift, then check both complete loop approaches, exits and nearby camera positions. The parent has identified a suitable opposite-drift relocation.

2. **P2 — tropical ferns intersect the ground and read as speckled debris.** `polishcritic-world0-start.png`, `polishcritic-world3-start.png`, `polishcritic-parrot-near-contact.png` and the matched jungle menu repeatedly show flat, broken dark green fragments. The current plant anchors in `world-detail.js` are at course Y minus 1.6/1.7 m, while nearby verge terrain is higher; the low curved frond shape then passes through the surface. Derive the anchor from the actual verge height or raise it enough to expose a deliberately upright cluster. Verify a clean near-camera silhouette and rooted grass/flowers rather than adding more instances.

3. **P2 — the dominant jungle cliffs still look like giant bare rounded boulders.** `polishcritic-world3-menu.png`, `polishcritic-world3-start.png` and `polishcritic-boost.png` make this limitation prominent. The newly added moss tiers are mostly placed inside the dodecahedral rock envelopes, so they do not visibly break the contour. Replace or compose the dominant shapes as asymmetric stacked outcrops with projecting vegetated ledges, an exposed face and irregular upper silhouettes. Ensure shelves sit beyond the underlying rock surface. Add coherent tree/plant clusters on visible ledges and connect those motifs to the gorge/gates, while retaining a clear road. A visible menu/start/bird-vista difference is required; another noisy texture alone does not address the composition.

4. **P2 — large grass/water surfaces show conspicuous repeated wave/checker patterns.** `polishcritic-world3-start.png` and `polishcritic-loop-exit.png` show turquoise water as a repeated grid, and verge surfaces as wavy stripes. Attenuate their large-scale albedo repetition, keep fine roughness/bump restrained and make water ripples directional and low contrast. The improved sky and character should remain the visual focus. A few authored foam/shallow-water forms are more effective than a blanket noisy grid.

## Improvements that are already visible

- Sonic is recognizably improved: rounded swept quills replace the old straight spikes; the unified face/eye shape, green pupils, nose glint and asymmetric smile read at the menu size. Gloves have seams/fingers and cuffs; shoes have soles, straps and buckles. Run poses show bent knees and distinct feet; jump and boost poses were observed. The procedural model remains toy-like and is not commercial character fidelity, but this is a clear silhouette and surface-detail improvement.
- Emerald Coast has a stronger layered checker-cliff identity, individual palm leaflets/coconuts, a coastal arch and textured road. The ring lines and hazard spikes remain legible. Sunken ferns and repeated terrain pattern are its principal new art defects.
- Sunset Ruins has warm pink/lilac atmospheric separation, stratified terraced cliffs, masonry arches/columns and small paving/mosaic fragments. The lane remains readable. Its pillars and broad canyon geometry are still repetitive but do communicate the world motif better than an undifferentiated flat scene.
- Starlight Circuit shows a star field, distinct rows of windows, panel seams, facade trim, rooftop equipment and cyan road edges. Character/track silhouettes and rings remain readable against the night palette. The distant simple pyramid forms and repeated rectangular towers remain obvious procedural placeholders; future refinement should replace those with a more varied skyline rather than brighter bloom.
- The loop crown/rise/descent/exit retain a continuous visible road and camera inversion. The character is visibly grounded, the gold rings and alternating curb accents stand out, and the sky remains behind the course. The critical entrance obstruction is separate from the otherwise readable loop traversal.
- Parrot warning UI, visible airborne bird and dive direction remain clear. The authored feather/color/beak/face improvements are source-confirmed; at racing scale the visible red/blue/yellow silhouette is the stronger contribution. This pass did not inspect the macaw as a stationary close-up asset.

## Performance, portrait and bounds

The first-loop sample returned 58 FPS near the crown and 64–86 through the other timed captures. Draw-call diagnostics ranged about 891–1119 at the starts and 408–933 in the super sequence; these include the configured render/shadow/post passes and are not a standalone scene-mesh count. Screenshots and instrumentation perturb timing; this is evidence of local browser responsiveness, not a hardware-independent benchmark or a sustained minimum-frame-rate guarantee.

At 390 × 844 the menu fits horizontally (`scrollWidth` 390), the cards are reachable by vertical scroll, the race HUD remains legible, the approach/ring lines/spikes remain visible and the jump pose is clear. The menu's enlarged character/sign overlap the title and selection background, but primary action text remains readable. Portrait race-start diagnostics returned 71 FPS. This used a desktop browser resized to portrait: actual phone GPU, touch interaction and physical controller hardware are untested. No full multi-minute race was required for this visual pass; course/physics regression tests belong to the parent's validation.

## Second-pass review — PASS

The critic reran all four desktop menus, starts and jump poses at 1440 × 900 on settled source. Fresh screenshots use `output/playwright/polishcritic-round2-*.png`, preserving all first-pass evidence. The first loop was traversed again through entry/rise/crown/descent/exit and the first parrot warning/swoop/boost. Ordinary race progression continued to the second loop, whose approach, entry, rise, crown, descent and actual post-exit road were captured. The final portrait menu, scrolled card list, race and jump were refreshed at 390 × 844. No game source or diagnostic state was changed by this critic.

- **Rock obstruction resolved.** The strongest direct comparison is `polishcritic-loop-entry.png` at timer 4.14 versus `polishcritic-round2-loop-entry.png` at 4.12. The old rock hides the player and central road; the revised view has a fully visible Sonic, road, ring line, curb and rail. The outcrop is beyond the right edge of the course. The source now uses the opposite side from the loop drift and frame-relative forward/up offsets. The second-loop approach/entry images also show the decorative outcrop safely beyond the verge, and both crowns, descents and exits remain readable. `polishcritic-round2-second-loop-after-exit.png` confirms clear road after leaving the second loop, not merely its final descending segment.
- **Fern intersections resolved.** The new `world0-start` and `world3-start` images have exposed leaf fans and upright clusters rather than the old broken surface fragments. Ground grass roots are raised. Clusters cast readable leaf shadows and remain outside the racing lane. Individual thin leaflets remain visibly procedural, but the terrain-intersection defect is no longer present in the exercised views.
- **Jungle composition revised visibly.** The menu, first-loop vista, first-bird view and second-loop approach now have stepped cliff contours, exposed green caps and trees/bush groups at the tops. This is a real silhouette/composition difference from the large rounded boulders, with the source matching the exposed top-face placement. The terraces are still stylized and repeated; future work could vary profile and scale more and refine bush silhouettes. These are fidelity limits rather than blockers for this improvement pass.
- **Water and ground repetition calmed.** In matched start/exit views the former turquoise grid and dominant wavy green pattern are subdued. Water now supports the course silhouette instead of competing with it. The sand still has a fine procedural texture; its lane markings, rings and hazard spikes remain easy to distinguish.
- **Existing improvements preserved.** The recognizable expressive menu character, curved running quills, glove/shoe detail, clear jump silhouette, city star/window/panel detail, ruins warm palette/masonry and coastal checker terraces survive the revision. Night-course screenshots show readable dark blue road, cyan rails, character and gold rings. The parrot warning and visible airborne red/blue/yellow bird remain readable.
- **Portrait regression passed.** Menu actions and scrolling card selection remain usable, race/jump/HUD fit the 390 px width and `scrollWidth` is 390. The known background character/sign overlap remains cosmetic. As before, viewport resizing is not a real touch-device test.

Second-pass desktop start diagnostics were 118/120/118/77 FPS for coast/ruins/city/jungle; menu samples were 118–120. First-loop/parrot/boost samples were 111–119 FPS, second-loop samples 95–116 and the final portrait start sample 88. These are local, instrumented samples with screenshot overhead, not sustained minimum-frame-rate measurements. The difference from first-pass samples should not be presented as an independently established performance gain.

No new blocking visual issue was found in the second pass. The final visual gate is satisfied. This remains an authored procedural fan game with a clear and noticeable character/world improvement; commercial asset fidelity, phone hardware and physical controller behavior are not claimed. Audio listening and production packaging remain the parent's separate review responsibilities.
