SONIC RING RUSH
A small, unofficial Sonic fan adventure for Windows browsers.

1. Extract the whole ZIP into a folder.
2. Double-click Start-Game.cmd.
3. Choose a world, then LET'S RACE.

No installation, Node.js, account, or internet connection is needed.
Use an up-to-date Chrome or Edge browser with hardware acceleration.
The Windows launcher opens a local-only server. It closes itself about
five minutes after the last game tab is closed. Keep the game folder intact.

CONTROLS
Sonic runs forward automatically.
Left / Right arrows or A / D: steer
Space: jump (release and press again for the next jump)
Shift: boost (collect rings to refill it)
Down arrow or S: brake
Esc: pause / resume

Controller: left stick or D-pad steer; A jump; X or RT boost; B brake;
Start pause. Activate the game once with the mouse to enable sound.
Touch buttons are available on devices with a touch pointer.

WORLD TOUR
Emerald Coast: sunny islands, palms, and checkerboard cliffs.
Sunset Ruins: sandstone arches and golden canyon climbs.
Starlight Circuit: neon bridges and a city under the stars.
Parrot Paradise: a 3.1 km super-track with two rideable vertical loops,
banked jungle bends, a waterfall bridge, canopy tunnels, fallen logs,
and swinging pendulums. Seven cheeky parrots dive from roadside trees.
When a parrot chirps, its target lane is marked: change lanes or jump!
Birds lock onto your lane at the warning, so moving afterward evades them.
Sonic grips the track through loops automatically; jump again after exiting.
Parrot knocks are recoverable. Little legends makes them gentler.
Every race has two laps and three computer-controlled rivals.
Little legends has gentler rivals; Speed stars rewards smart boosts.
Gold, silver, and bronze mark finishing place. Best times and medals
are saved in this browser on this PC; different browsers keep separate saves.

All character geometry, scenery, effects, and music were created in code.
Each world now has its own original, arranged soundtrack. Emerald Coast and
Parrot Paradise feature Caribbean steel drums and island percussion.
Use Sound settings in the menu or pause screen to adjust Music and Sound
effects separately. The note button mutes everything. Settings are saved.
